How to play

You are tied together by a red string. Nobody gets home alone — both of you must reach the heart arch.

Online (each on your own computer)

  • AD / ←→ move
  • W / ↑ / Space jump (hold = higher)
  • S / ↓ hold to anchor
  • E / Q hold to climb / pull the string
  • 1–4 emotes ❤️ 👋 ✋ ⬆️
  • K kiss 💋 (stand right next to each other)

Same keyboard

  • P1: WASD · climb E · kiss F · emotes 1–4
  • P2: ←↑↓→ · climb R-Shift or / · kiss L · emotes 7–0

Tricks

  • 🪨 Anchor on solid ground to hold your partner when they fall.
  • 🧗 Hold climb to shorten the string and pull yourself up to an anchored partner.
  • 🧍 Stand on your partner's head to reach high ledges.
  • 🧱 Jump at a wall and hold anchor to grab it — make yourself a step for your partner.
  • 🚩 Flags save your spot. If one of you falls, you both go back.
